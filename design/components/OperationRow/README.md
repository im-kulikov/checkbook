# OperationRow

An operation row in the feed, in a category, in «Входящие» ("Inbox") and in the payee card.

- On the left is the author's avatar; the meta line has the full category path and the time or date.
- A part of a split receipt shows its share: «8 000 ₽ · из чека 12 500» ("8 000 ₽ · of receipt 12 500").
- A refund is «+» in the `success` colour with the «возврат» ("refund") tag; a transfer to one's own is in the `text-2` colour with «не в итогах» ("not in totals"); a draft has a `warning` tag.
- An unsent operation is labelled «не отправлено» ("not sent") in text, not only with an icon.
